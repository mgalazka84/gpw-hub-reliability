Analysis provenance and limits of the archival record

PROTOCOL.txt records the Rosenberg source, complete-adult inclusion rule,
reverse coding, 20 percent training fraction and split seed 61023117.
The public history first adds this protocol, the simulation design and the
application results together in commit
9a34c2b6fa4f9bb71364dd9fe63d82956f05e611 (2026-10-26T09:47:54Z).
There is no earlier public protocol commit in this branch's history.
The repository therefore does not independently establish that these choices,
or the designation of FS-T64 as primary, preceded the application results.
No external preregistration was made. The original files are retained as
historical records rather than retrospectively rewritten as registrations.

The application is exploratory. All 20 variants are reported without
retrospective primary/secondary ranking. Each mathematical guarantee concerns
one fixed procedure, not selection among methods based on application results.
The extra 100 splits are a post-application descriptive stability check.
Their design is written before the new runs, but is not a prospective public
registration. No split is selected for replacement of the reported split.
Results are not pooled to produce a stronger confidence claim.
